We’re betting big on AI, so deep LLM observability is non-negotiable

How we’re putting AI to work

  1. 1Productivity. Helps a person with a task.
  2. 2Processes. Coordinates daily work.
  3. 3Value streams. Connects work end to end.

Human in the loop, by design

Every material decision has a human owner, an audit trail and a way to roll it back.

What we need to see across every model, frontier and in-house

  • Cost and token consumption per use case
  • Latency and availability of model endpoints
  • Answer quality, hallucinations and drift
  • Security: prompt injection and data leakage
  • End-to-end traceability of every agent action

Sources: Banco Pichincha, CLAB 2026 presentation (October 2026); Banco Pichincha Technology priorities.
